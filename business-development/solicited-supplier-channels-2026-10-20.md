## Compliance / delivery gates

1. CantaX currently operates as an *independent solo engineering initiative*, not as an incorporated company. Never claim incorporation, ISO certification, protected professional sign-off, manufacturing approval, production projects, staff, proven lead times, installed clients or a VAT ID not actually held.
2. In Spain, LSSI Law 34/2002 (art. 21) generally prohibits unsolicited commercial email without prior request/authorization (subject to limited existing-customer exception). This cannot be bypassed just by emailing a foreign recipient or disguising an offer as a "question". Primary government guidance: https://lssi.digital.gob.es/lssi/la-ley/aspectos-basicos/publicidad-en-internet ; https://www.boe.es/buscar/act.php?id=BOE-A-2002-13758 .
3. A company advertising an *actual supplier onboarding/registration route* is distinct from an ordinary sales/customer-support email address. Use the route as stated and limit communication to its purpose; acceptance remains discretionary. Respect terms and don't complete unknown legal representations.
4. Do not email German or Dutch generic addresses without consent/qualifying invitation. UK ICO PECR has a corporate-subscriber exception but Spanish origin rules need accounting for. US CAN-SPAM imposes postal-address and opt-out duties on commercial email, including B2B. The user's business postal address has not been verified.
5. Never use personal Gmail as the CantaX sender without express approval. The CantaX Gmail browser profile setup must be completed by the owner.
6. An explicit new-supplier form is permission to make a relevant application according to its terms, **not blanket consent for sales emails or unrelated communications**.
7. Preserve each date, source URL, route, content, actual submission identifier and reply. No "contacted" CRM status until real submission/send confirmed.
