## Suggested sequence

1. Follow 6–8 **A-priority company pages** as CantaX; do not mass-follow indiscriminately.
2. Complete an original, downloadable engineering CAD portfolio with correct assumptions, drawings, BOM and STEP exports; no customer IP.
3. Look for specific evidence of drawing backlog, projects, procurement announcements, design-for-manufacture issues or requests for suppliers.
4. Prepare a tailored, accurate 2–3 sentence message to the appropriate engineering or procurement contact. Follow platform anti-spam policies and GDPR/direct-marketing rules.
5. Record contact dates and responses; **never invent referrals, certifications, employees or completed customer projects**.
