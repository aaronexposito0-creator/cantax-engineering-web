## Service offer: technical pilot, not generic “cheap engineering”

A small test assignment is proposed only after buyer confirmation:

1. **Approved-input CAD conversion / revision** — client supplies a dimensioned part or assembly reference with use rights; we agree a single revised model / neutral file and a revision explanation.
2. **Drawing quality / BOM clean-up** — compare two authorized technical lists; report added, removed and changed parts, data issues and checks; include reproducible CSV and source logic.
3. **Small marine equipment detail** — one non-safety-critical mounting/interface study with a scope, geometry assumptions, reference BOM and technical questions for the client's engineer.

**Review gates**: file licenses, commercial CAD software availability, client IP, data-transfer method, manufacturing tolerances, approval responsibility, legal supplier qualification, tax/invoicing setup, and realistic hours. No promise of fitness for use, precision, turnaround or approvals without scope validation.

## Tracking guidance

Before a contact is attempted, record source URL, date verified, recipient role, supplier channel suitability, country e-privacy review, specific proposed deliverables and a yes/no reason for contacting. Record 'contacted' only after an actual confirmed send. Stop after an opt-out/no interest. Never present a draft, LinkedIn company follow, or unverified need as a commercial lead.
