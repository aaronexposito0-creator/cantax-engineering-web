## Positioning and what is being sold

> **Scoped CAD, drawing/documentation and engineering workflow support — remotely delivered from Spain, with straightforward handover and lean overhead.**

Not: “we're cheaper than everyone” or “all designs are fabrication-ready.” We need to demonstrate value by accepting one small, objectively checkable task, selecting the right commercial CAD tools and agreeing the acceptance criteria.

### Three starting micro-scopes for a client discussion

**A. Drawing-to-model pilot**: one existing part from approved dimensioned reference, 3D geometry, exchange format, revision note. Excludes novel mechanism design, bespoke stress approval and confidential uploads via the website.

**B. Small drawing pack pilot**: one reviewed part or small assembly with known datums/tolerances supplied by client, PDF and optional DXF, parts register.

**C. BOM / technical-data cleanup**: compare two approved CSV BOMs, classify additions/deletions/revisions and produce a repeatable check script and explanation.

All three are **quotations on request**. For learning, estimate **internally** €120–300 for a genuinely small task and €250–600 for a properly scoped mini-tool; these are planning hypotheses, not public or binding tariffs. Never promise turnaround until complexity, file access and available hours are checked. The prototype case studies are not proof of manufacturing accuracy.

## Qualification before sending anything

1. Confirm business is trading and has the technical engineering services indicated on its current official website.
2. Confirm a published, appropriate **business collaboration, partnerships or procurement contact** rather than an unrelated sales/support inbox.
3. Find one concrete technical reason the relevant company might benefit from a scoped task.
4. Verify whether a legal basis/consent and national e-privacy rules permit unsolicited business correspondence (especially Germany); where not, use a vendor signup or requested introduction.
5. Ensure CantaX's website is on hosting allowing commercial use, accurate legal/privacy disclosures, and a legal invoicing structure ready before commercial contracts.
6. Customise each email with a genuine 1–2 sentence reference; avoid personal-data enrichment beyond what is necessary.
7. Allow a human to review before any message is sent. No automated mass-messaging / no duplicate follow-ups to nonconsenting recipients.

## First controlled commercial experiment (4 weeks)

- **Week 1:** Finish/legal review of site and deliverables; choose 5 realistic accounts; research procurement channels; refine one client-facing CAD sample.
- **Week 2:** With contact lawfulness checked, send up to **5 individualized, relevant messages**, not automated bulk emails. Record results.

### Measured funnel and stopping rules

Record for each qualified contact: company, target, link to evidence, email channel, proposed scope, message subject, date, response, quote, duration estimate, legal/rejection flags.

Aim for **10 genuinely qualified messages in month one**, not 1,000 generic messages. If zero interested replies, adjust offer and prospect quality before scaling. No “guaranteed clients” forecast.
