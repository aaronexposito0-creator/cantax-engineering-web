02 / COMPUTED OUTPUTS

Different questions. Different answers.

Nominal stack—Signed arithmetic sum of nominal dimensions
Worst-case interval—Exact envelope when all specified bounds are respected
Uniform-model σ—Independent errors uniformly distributed within ± tolerances
Simulated outside limits—12,000 synthetic samples, not an actual shop-floor reject rate

Monte Carlo illustration · fixed seed

Each dimension's error is sampled independently from its stated ± tolerance interval, with equal probability. Actual manufacturing processes may behave differently.

03 / METHOD & LIMITATIONS

Clear assumptions. No inflated claims.

Analytical method

Nominal = sum of signed dimensions. Worst-case range = nominal ± sum of all tolerances. Uniform-model standard deviation = square root of the sum of each squared tolerance divided by three.

Not included

No geometric dimensioning and tolerancing (GD&T), process correlation, temperature, wear, fit verification, structural strength, non-uniform process distributions or production certification.
