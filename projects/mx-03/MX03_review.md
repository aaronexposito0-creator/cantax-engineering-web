# MX-03 preliminary engineering review — scope limits

## Design problem

A marine instrument is assumed to require a deployable position away from a platform edge. A pivoting arm gives rotational reach while permitting stowage for maintenance.

## Key unresolved engineering questions (REQUIRED BEFORE DESIGN FREEZE)

1. Instrument mass and submerged mass, buoyancy, sensor vibration and dynamic payload loads.
2. Wave impact/slamming, ship motions, expected design sea state, spray, wind loads and operational cycles.
3. Actuation or manual handling arrangement, locking system, fail-safe retention and physical hard stops.
4. Pivot pin bearing surface, radial/axial loads, fatigue, seals and maintainability.
5. Weld group loads, frame/member strength and actual deck structure; differential deflection under operation.
6. Selected metallic grades, insulation across dissimilar metals and realistic ISO 12944 protective system specification by exposure scenario.
7. Cable bend radius, strain relief, glands, EMC and waterproof interface.
8. Drawing standard, inspection hold points, dimensional tolerances and structural compliance pathways.

This portfolio package includes only kinematics and concept CAD, intentionally avoiding invented strength and certification claims. The concept shall NOT be fabricated or installed based solely on these documents.

## Geometric insights

The tip moves on a circular arc about the pivot. Increasing angle decreases reach beyond the deck while increasing elevation; a designer must check both reach and clearance at every intermediate angle. The 12° stowed arm projects significantly beyond the 1250 mm supporting deck frame, so a bespoke park geometry would be required for a truly compact stowage.

## Client project application

A real scope could start with an equipment interface review, requirements matrix, preliminary GA, risk/assumptions log and supervised dimensioned CAD under agreed checking responsibilities.
