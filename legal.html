Privacy and forms

The project brief is prepared in your browser. Copying or downloading it does not transmit information to CantaX. If you choose “Open email app”, your email provider handles the message and you must send it yourself. The optional “Send enquiry online” feature is disabled until a Web3Forms integration has been activated and independently tested. Once enabled, choosing it will transfer your contact details and project description over HTTPS to Web3Forms for forwarding to cantaxengineering@gmail.com. Do not submit passwords, personal identifiers, sensitive data, client-confidential drawings or regulated technical information through this channel. A provider response alone is not proof of email arrival; end-to-end receipt should be verified.

This initial static website does not install analytics trackers or cookies. External links, including email services, may apply their own policies.
